About us:

"We are four comp sci High students named Jamilet, Lovaya, Geanna and Ethel, who created a website called Community Watch. Our goals is to help people stay informed about what dangers are happening in their community and feel safer in their neighborhood."

"We designed Community Watch for a design competition but we hope it can become something that makes a real difference in the Bronx community. Our website is designed to help people stay aware, connected and prepared while encouraging everyone to look out for one another we believe that a safer community starts with an informed community."
