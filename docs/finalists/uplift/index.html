Our Commitment

Our Mission at UPLIFT — BX Innovators

At UPLIFT - BX Innovators, our core mission is to eliminate homelessness and hunger across our South Bronx neighborhoods by providing immediate, dignified access to life-sustaining shelter and food resources.

We build accessible, community-first digital tools that connect vulnerable individuals and families directly with real-time shelter bed availability, local food pantries, daily warm meal schedules, and youth drop-in centers.
