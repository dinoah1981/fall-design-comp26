Know Your Rights in NYC

1. You have the right to remain silent

You do not have to answer questions about your citizenship or status. Say: "I am exercising my right to remain silent."

2. Do not open the door without a warrant

Agents must show a judicial warrant signed by a judge with your exact name and address. Ask them to slide it under the door.

3. You have the right to record video

In NYC public spaces, you have the right to record video provided you do not physically obstruct officers.

4. Do not sign documents without legal counsel

Never sign anything you do not understand. You have the right to consult an attorney.
